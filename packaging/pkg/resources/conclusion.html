ZTab is running

Its icon is in the menu bar. The first time, ZTab opens a Set up ZTab window for its two permissions:

  • Accessibility (required): to catch ⌥Tab and switch windows,
  • Screen Recording (optional): for window thumbnails.

Click Grant… next to each, then turn on ZTab in the list System Settings shows. macOS may also say that a background item was added: that is ZTab's start at login.

Settings (show delay, shortcuts, start at login) are in the menu bar icon's menu.
